# Features (20)

Things real sites have that vibe coded sites skip. Mark each one Done, Not needed (say why), or Missing.

1. **Dark mode toggle.** A light and dark switch that starts from the visitor's system setting and remembers their choice. Both modes pass contrast.
   Not needed if: the brand only works in one mode. Say so in the report.
2. **Announcement banner.** A thin bar across the top for one timely message (an offer, new hours, a holiday closure) that visitors can close.
   Not needed if: there's nothing timely to say. Never invent urgency.
3. **Site search.** For sites with lots of pages, posts, or products.
   Not needed if: the site has five pages or fewer.
4. **Back to top button.** Shows up after scrolling down a long page. Works with a keyboard.
5. **Mobile menu.** A real menu on phones that opens and closes with a tap, keeps focus inside while open, and closes with Escape.
6. **Loading states.** Skeletons or spinners for anything that loads, so nothing looks broken. Quick and subtle.
7. **Hover states.** Every link and button visibly reacts on hover and on keyboard focus.
8. **Scroll progress bar.** A thin bar that shows how far through a long page someone is.
   Not needed if: every page is short.
9. **Copy button.** One tap to copy what people copy: an email address, a phone number, a discount code, a code snippet.
   Not needed if: there's nothing worth copying.
10. **Print stylesheet.** Pages print clean: no navigation, no buttons, readable text.
    Not needed if: nobody prints this site. Menus, recipes, quotes, and guides do get printed.
11. **Sticky header.** Navigation and the main button stay reachable while scrolling, without covering the content.
12. **Skip to content link.** The first thing a keyboard user can tab to. It jumps past the navigation.
13. **Password visibility toggle.** A show and hide button on every password field.
    Not needed if: the site has no logins.
14. **UTM tracking.** Links in posts, emails, and ads carry UTM tags, so analytics show which channel brought in each call.
    Not needed if: the site has no analytics.
15. **Form success state.** After someone submits, they see a clear message that says what happens next and when.
16. **Form error state.** Errors show up next to the field, say how to fix it, and never wipe what they typed.
17. **Confirmation modal.** Anything destructive or hard to undo (cancel a booking, delete an account) asks "Are you sure?" first.
    Not needed if: nothing on the site is destructive.
18. **Last updated date.** On legal pages, guides, and anything time sensitive.
19. **Expandable FAQ.** Questions that open and close, work with a keyboard, and are marked up so search engines can read them.
20. **Floating contact button.** A small button in the corner that goes straight to the goal: book, call, or message.
