# Before launch (20)

Run this right before the site goes live. Mark each one Done, Not needed (say why), or Missing.

## The goal

1. **Call to action above the fold.** The main button is visible without scrolling, on desktop and on phones.
2. **Sticky mobile call to action.** On phones, the goal button stays pinned to the bottom of the screen.
3. **Response time promise.** Next to the button and the form: "We reply within one business day." Only promise what they can keep.
4. **Thank you page.** After someone books or submits, send them to a real page that confirms it, says what happens next, and gives them something useful while they wait.
5. **Custom 404 page.** A friendly page with the main button and links back to the good stuff, instead of a dead end.

## Trust

6. **Real reviews.** Near the call to action, with names, and linked to the source (Google, Yelp) when possible.
7. **Case studies.** One to three real examples: the problem, what they did, the result.
   Not needed if: they're brand new. Show a clear process section instead, and never invent a case study.
8. **Team photo.** Real faces of the real people. It's one of the strongest trust signals a small business has.
9. **Five FAQs.** The five questions customers actually ask, answered plainly. Use the expandable FAQ from the features list.
10. **Maps and directions.** An embedded map, the address, and a directions link.
    Not needed if: customers never visit in person.

## Findable

11. **Unique page titles.** Every page has its own title, under about 60 characters, with the business name.
12. **Meta descriptions.** Every page has its own one or two sentence summary, under about 155 characters.
13. **Social share image.** A 1200 by 630 image with Open Graph and Twitter card tags, so links look good when people share them.
14. **Internal links.** Pages link to each other where it helps: a service to its case study, the FAQ to contact.
15. **Breadcrumbs.** On sites with nested pages, show visitors where they are.
    Not needed if: every page is one click from home.
16. **robots.txt.** It exists, lets search engines in, and points to the sitemap. Make sure it isn't still blocking everything from a staging setup.
17. **Local business schema.** Structured data with the name, address, phone, hours, and service area, so search engines understand the business.
    Not needed if: they aren't a local business. Use organization schema instead.
18. **Alt text on images.** Check it again on the final content. (Also on the trust checklist.)

## Measure and protect

19. **Privacy policy page.** Live and linked in the footer. (Also on the trust checklist.)
20. **Google Analytics.** Or a privacy friendly option like Plausible, Fathom, or Vercel Analytics, with the booking or form submission tracked as a conversion. Set it up only after the consent steps from the trust checklist are in place.
