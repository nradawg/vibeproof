# Trust, legal, and accessibility (19)

What keeps a site honest, legal, and usable by everyone. Mark each one Done, Not needed (say why), or Missing.

This isn't legal advice. The rules depend on where the business and its visitors are. When something matters, tell them to check with a professional.

## Accessibility

1. **Color contrast.** Body text 4.5:1, large text and buttons 3:1, in light and dark mode. See `color.md`.
2. **Alt text on images.** Every meaningful image says what it shows. Decorative images get empty alt text (`alt=""`).
3. **Fix accessibility issues.** Run an automated check (Lighthouse, axe, or WAVE) and fix what it finds. Then use the whole site with only a keyboard.
4. **Clear button labels.** Buttons say what they do ("Book a call", not "Submit"). Icon only buttons get an accessible name.
5. **Keyboard friendly forms.** Every field has a visible label, a logical tab order, and a visible focus ring. The form works without a mouse.

## Legal pages

6. **Privacy policy page.** What data is collected, why, who it's shared with, and how to get it deleted. It matches what the site actually does.
7. **Terms and conditions page.** The rules for using the site and buying from the business.
8. **Refund policy.** How refunds and cancellations work.
   Not needed if: they don't sell anything online.
9. **Cookie policy.** Which cookies and trackers the site uses, and why.
   Not needed if: the site sets no cookies beyond the ones it strictly needs.
10. **Cookie consent.** If the site uses analytics or ad cookies, ask before loading them where the law requires it (the EU and UK, for example). Saying no is as easy as saying yes.
11. **Form consent.** Forms say what happens with the data. Marketing emails get an unchecked opt in box, never a pre-checked one.
12. **Check local laws.** Privacy, accessibility, and consumer rules differ by country and state (GDPR, CCPA, the ADA, and others). Flag the ones that apply to where they are and who they serve.

## Data

13. **Check tracking.** List every script that tracks visitors. Remove anything unused, and make sure the privacy policy covers the rest.
14. **Only collect necessary data.** Every form field earns its place. For booking a call, that's usually name, email, and a short message.
15. **Check third party embeds.** Maps, videos, chat widgets, and booking tools bring their own trackers and slow the page down. Keep only what helps the goal, and disclose it.

## Honesty

16. **Remove fake reviews.** No invented testimonials, no "Jane Doe", no stock photo customers. Only real reviews, ideally with a name and a link to where they were posted.
17. **Remove unsupported claims.** No "#1 in town", "trusted by thousands", or made up stats unless they can prove it.
18. **Add real business details.** Legal business name, address or service area, phone, email, and hours. Real details build trust with people and with search engines.
19. **Check image copyright.** Every image is theirs, licensed, or from a source that allows commercial use. Nothing pulled from a Google image search.
