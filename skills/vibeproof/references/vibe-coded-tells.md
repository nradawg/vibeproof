# Vibe coded tells

People can spot an AI built site in seconds. Scan for these and remove every one.

## Looks

- Purple to blue gradients and glowing blobs behind the hero
- Gradient text on headings
- Three identical cards in a row, each with an icon, a title, and two lines of text
- Small all caps labels above every single section
- 01, 02, 03 numbers on sections that aren't actually steps
- A thin border plus a big soft shadow on every card
- Cards rounded more than about 16px
- A different bright color on every card
- Fake screenshots built out of divs, or hand drawn SVG "art" in place of real images
- Stock photos of people laughing at laptops
- The same default font on everything. Inter everywhere is a giveaway, so pick fonts on purpose.

## Copy

- Em dashes all over the copy
- Buzzwords like seamless, elevate, unlock, empower, game changer, cutting edge, robust, and supercharge
- "It's not just X, it's Y" lines, and lists of exactly three in every sentence
- Fake numbers, fake testimonials, and placeholder names like "Jane Doe"
- Headlines that could belong to any business, like "Innovative solutions for your needs"

## Behavior

- Buttons that go nowhere and links with no real URL
- Hover effects with no keyboard focus state
- Focus rings removed
- Placeholder text used instead of labels
- Animations that move layout (width, height, top, left) instead of transform and opacity
- No reduced motion fallback for people who turn animations off
- Everything fading in on scroll, all at once, every time

## The fix, in short

- One accent color, used only for the goal
- Two font families at most, a clear size scale, and body text at 16px or bigger
- Spacing from one scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96
- Real photos of the real business
- Plain words a customer would actually say
